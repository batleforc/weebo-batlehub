---
title: CRD reference
description: Every kind the operator adds, its fields, and an interactive manifest builder.
---

Each page lists a kind's `spec` fields, shows a minimal example, and has a
form that builds a ready-to-apply manifest. These pages are generated from
the CRDs themselves, so they always match the installed version.

| Kind | Short names | Scope | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [`BatleHubLocalInstance`](/docs/crds/batlehublocalinstance) | `bhli` | Namespaced | One BatleHub instance, owned by a namespace: its token is that namespace's, and only the objects of that namespace use it (plus the `BatleHubRegistry`s of the namespaces it accepts). |
| [`BatleHubRegistry`](/docs/crds/batlehubregistry) | `bhr` | Namespaced | One `[[registries]]` entry of an instance's configuration, as its own object, so each team manages its registries. |
| [`BatleHubNamespaceClaim`](/docs/crds/batlehubnamespaceclaim) | `bhnc` | Namespaced | A team namespace of a registry: a package name prefix reserved to a group of the instance's auth providers. |
| [`BatleHubPackageGrant`](/docs/crds/batlehubpackagegrant) | `bhpg` | Namespaced | The grants of a package, or of one version of it, in a registry of the instance: who may do what there, beyond what the instance's and the registry's configuration grant (`grants`, `registries[].grants`). |
| [`BatleHubPackagePolicy`](/docs/crds/batlehubpackagepolicy) | `bhpp` | Namespaced | The policy of a package, or of one version of it, in a registry of the instance, and the exemptions of a version from its gates. |
