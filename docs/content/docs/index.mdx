---
title: weebo-batlehub
description: A Kubernetes operator that drives BatleHub instances as CRDs.
---

`weebo-batlehub` drives [BatleHub](https://github.com/batlehub/batlehub),
the package registry proxy-cache, from Kubernetes: its instances, and in
the coming releases its configuration, its registries, the grants and
policies of its packages, and its maintenance tasks.

<Callout type="warn">
  Early days: this release connects a `BatleHubLocalInstance` and checks
  that the operator's token is accepted with the admin role. Nothing is
  released yet; the first alpha comes with configuration management.
</Callout>
