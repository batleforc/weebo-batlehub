---
title: weebo-batlehub
description: A Kubernetes operator that drives BatleHub instances as CRDs.
---

`weebo-batlehub` drives [BatleHub](https://github.com/batlehub/batlehub),
the package registry proxy-cache, from Kubernetes: its instances, their
configuration and registries, the team namespaces of its registries, the
grants and policies of its packages, and its maintenance tasks.

<Callout type="warn">
  Alpha: a `BatleHubLocalInstance` either connects an existing BatleHub
  (`external`, which with `manageConfig` pushes its configuration and its
  `BatleHubRegistry`s), or is deployed by the operator (`managed`).
</Callout>
