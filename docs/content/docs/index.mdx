---
title: weebo-batlehub
description: A Kubernetes operator that drives BatleHub instances as CRDs.
---

`weebo-batlehub` drives [BatleHub](https://github.com/batlehub/batlehub),
the package registry proxy-cache, from Kubernetes: its instances, their
configuration and registries, the team namespaces of its registries, and
in the coming releases the grants and policies of its packages, and its
maintenance tasks.

<Callout type="warn">
  Alpha: a `BatleHubLocalInstance` connects an existing BatleHub and, with
  `external.manageConfig`, pushes its configuration and its
  `BatleHubRegistry`s. The mode where the operator deploys BatleHub comes
  later.
</Callout>
